Also produce "agenda_review": exactly one entry per planned point, in the same order, none omitted:
- "n": the point's number
- "status": exactly one of
    "resolved"        an answer, decision or plan was agreed, or someone took the follow-up action on it
                      (the action itself may still be pending: it is then listed in "tasks")
    "discussed_open"  discussed substantively but left undecided, or explicitly postponed after the discussion
    "partial"         only briefly touched, or only part of the point was covered
    "not_discussed"   not talked about; a passing mention of a keyword is NOT a discussion
- "outcome": one short Russian sentence: what was concluded, or what is still open (null for not_discussed)
- "evidence_quote": a short verbatim quote from the meeting proving the discussion (required unless not_discussed)
Judge by what was actually said, not by the order of the agenda and not by the summary you wrote. A topic that was
mentioned and then explicitly postponed is not "resolved". If you really cannot tell whether anything was agreed, choose
discussed_open; if unsure between partial and not_discussed, choose not_discussed. Losing a topic is worse than
raising it twice.
Also produce "unplanned": significant topics that were discussed but were not on the agenda (short Russian phrases).
